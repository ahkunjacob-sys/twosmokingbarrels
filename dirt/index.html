DIRT

In rural Wayanad, a young boy pushed down one too many times fights back with a fistful of dirt.

Now on the festival circuit. Public release in 2027.

Runtime 8 min 12 sLanguage MalayalamSet in Wayanad, KeralaProduced by Malayil Foundations

01Synopsis

In a village in Wayanad, an eleven-year-old Adivasi boy runs to the playground to join a football game, only to be turned away. Over one afternoon he watches the same exclusion fall on his mother and father, until a final humiliation in a paddy field pushes him to answer back with the only thing he has.
