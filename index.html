What we believe

  • Picture and sound start in the same room.Music is not something added at the end. The score and the shot get written together, from the first idea.
  • The door stays open.Directors, composers, writers, editors, sound designers, actors, painters. If you make things, there is a seat here.
  • Make it now. Perfect it later.We would rather make something small this month than plan something big forever.
  • Every name goes on the credits.The collaboration belongs to everyone in it, and the credits say so.
